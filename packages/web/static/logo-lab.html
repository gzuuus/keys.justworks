keys.justworks / identity study 02

Nostr, cut into the key.

Ten production-minded marks built from one brief: fuse a user-held key with a recognisable Nostr signal. Every direction is drawn for silhouette, owns its negative space, and is shown at real interface sizes.

  • Key at first glance
  • Nostr encoded
  • Works at 20px
  • One-colour safe
01

N-key monogram

A precise Nostr “N” is locked into the bow; the diagonal becomes a conventional cut key.

02

Cut N

The Nostr initial is physically cut through a solid key bow, giving the mark a strong one-piece silhouette.

03

Relay key

Four relay nodes draw the N inside the bow. The protocol is the mechanism, not decoration.

04

Event key

A signed Nostr event is the key’s bow; its folded corner flows directly into the shaft.

05

Ostrich cut

The Nostr ostrich’s neck resolves into the key channel; its body becomes the grip.

06

Keypair N

Two cryptographic key heads anchor a single N. The public and private pair stay visibly connected.

07

Nostr stamp

A bold app-icon direction: the N is stamped into an orange key bow with an uncompromised small-size silhouette.

08

Note key

The open protocol’s note bubble becomes the grip; the signature exits as a cut key.

09

Five-relay N

Five identity nodes form an N inside the bow, directly connecting the landing animation to the permanent mark.

10

Only you / N

The strongest idea from round one, redrawn as an N-shaped key: one identity node, one owner, one exit.
